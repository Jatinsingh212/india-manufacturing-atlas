SLIDE 9  (slides/slide9.xml)  layout=['../slideLayouts/slideLayout78.xml']
[sp Title 1 ] Risk & mitigation measures 
[table Table 7 @(0.7,1.1 12.5x5.4)]
   || S. No. || Risk Area || Key Risk || Mitigation Measures
   || 1 || Data Integration || Multiple data sources (CBIC, GSTN, DGFT, Ministries) leading to inconsistencies and delays || Establish HSN-first data standard, build API-based pipelines, start with priority datasets
   || 2 || Data Quality & Reliability || Incomplete/incorrect production, GST, and cluster data || Use AI-based data validation, cross-check datasets, introduce data confidence scores, start with high-confidence trade data
   || 3 || Technology & Scalability || Handling large database (12,000+ HSN codes) and real-time analytics || Deploy cloud-based scalable architecture (Google Cloud), use modular design, perform load testing, follow phased rollout
   || 4 || Data Security & Privacy || Risk of sensitive data exposure or misuse || Implement role-based access control (RBAC), ensure encryption, follow government security standards, define clear access protocols
   || 5 || Value Chain Mapping Complexity || Difficulty in mapping input-output relationships across HSN codes || Start with pilot sectors, use AI/graph analytics, validate with industry experts, refine iteratively
   || 6 || Scope Creep / Over-Ambition || Trying to cover all sectors and features at once || Adopt phased implementation strategy
